UNICEF India · Child Health Indicators

India-only analysis of ARI care-seeking, ORS treatment, ITN use and immunization coverage using UNICEF source data.

The question

What was I exploring?

What do India’s reported child-health indicators show, and how do reference years differ?

Approach

How I approached it

Filter UNICEF Global Dataflow to India (IND), match six source indicator codes, retain the reporting year and compare historical observations.

Interpretation

What the evidence suggests

Latest selected values: ARI 56.1%, ORS 60.6%, child ITN use 4.4%, household ITN ownership 7.9% (2021); DTP3 and MCV2 95% (2025). Different reporting years must not be conflated.

Data integrity

The UNICEF indicators come from published source data. Reference years differ and descriptive patterns do not establish causality.
