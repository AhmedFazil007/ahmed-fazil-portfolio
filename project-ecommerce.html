Saudi E-Commerce & Last-Mile Delivery

A six-page analytical study of simulated orders, customers, sales, delivery performance, cancellations and returns.

Project visuals
The question

What was I exploring?

How can an e-commerce business identify sales-quality and last-mile delivery bottlenecks?

Approach

How I approached it

Model customer, product, order and order-item tables; validate transactions; calculate sales and delivery KPIs; compare growth, average order value and fulfillment performance.

Interpretation

What the evidence suggests

Order growth alone is not proof of stronger commercial performance. Compare delivered sales, average order value and late deliveries before recommending operational action.

Data integrity

This project uses synthetic data. Figures are simulated and do not describe actual Saudi market outcomes.
